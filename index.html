CARE FOR YOUR ROYAL PAIR

CARE FOR YOUR ROYAL PAIRMade by hand. Made to last.To keep your Royal Footwear looking sharp for years:1. Daily Care Wipe with a dry cloth after wear to remove dust. For leather shoes, use a soft brush to keep them clean.2. Polish & Condition Apply leather polish or conditioner once every 2-3 weeks. This keeps the leather soft and prevents cracking. Always use colors that match your shoe.3. Water & Heat Genuine leather doesn’t like water. If your shoes get wet, air-dry them away from direct sunlight or heat. Use a shoe tree to maintain shape.4. Storage Store in a cool, dry place. Use the dust bag we provide to protect from scratches.5. Sole Care Avoid dragging your feet. Get your soles checked by a cobbler every 6 months if you wear them daily.
